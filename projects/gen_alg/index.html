🚀 Rocket Evolution Lab

Interactive genetic algorithm simulation where rockets evolve to reach targets while avoiding obstacles.

Controls (Desktop):

Shift + Click → Add Target

Shift + Right Click → Delete Target

Drag → Draw obstacle

Right Drag → Erase obstacle

Drag targets → Move them around

Controls (Mobile):

Tap → Add Target

Drag → Draw obstacle

Long Press + Drag → Delete objects
