Journey of Music.

Getting started here

I started listening to music quite a while ago, from some TV channels. Which was heavily influenced from different genres during the COVID. From then, I have been listening to music as ambience for focusing on any hobbies, or even studying.

I thought i should learn a new instrument, as it also improves focus and dexterity. So I picked Piano for this, and hell, it's tough. It requires ambidextrous movement, sometimes indepenent. And it also needs consistent practice, so i'm quite dull in it. (failure as an Asian)
I have danced a lot, in cultural functions, and I have also sung sometimes. But I wish I had a better voice:).

Future References

I would try learning ukelele, if i could learn any new instruments. Also learning to read sheet music would be dope. Learning to play any song i want in the Piano would be awesome too!
For ambient music, I would suggest "Nobody" on youtube. Also, for learning music theory, you can visit "Synthet7" on youtube too.
