Getting started here

The only tips and tricks at getting better at drawing, is to actually draw, consistently. It can be anything you see in your daily life. And for learning, free online resources are there in Youtube and Pinterest.

Extra tips

• Avoid Art Block
• Avoid tutorial hell
• Don't be disappointed in your work.
• Study other peoples' works(Not copy).

References

Craft Handmade

The Black Pearl

Inspired from the franchise "Pirates of the Carribean", this craft was made for the Creativity Exhibition 2025. After working and planning for over a week, this masterpiece was created, which ended up securing Champion in the competition. It is also to be mentioned, many juniors also helped me in building this model. And moreover, it is now in display in the College Museum in FCC.

This project used many daily-life items for its construction. My favorite thing about this, is the wood texture was made from cartridge paper that was colored accordingly. Also the shiny texture comes from varnish, that is used to restore paintings:)

Craft Handmade

Popup Collections

Paper crafts are the creative practice of shaping paper into decorative or functional designs through folding, cutting, and layering. They include origami, collages, and handmade objects, showing how a simple sheet can be transformed into expressive art with minimal tools.

Pop‑ups and paper engineering extend this tradition by adding mechanics and movement. Pop‑ups create interactive structures that rise into three dimensions when opened, while paper engineering provides the technical precision behind these effects—using geometry and design to build complex, kinetic works.
