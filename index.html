Multiverse Concept

One mind, many parallel timelines of making.

Instead of treating hobbies as side-notes, this portfolio treats each domain as a fully fledged universe. As you scroll, you move between dimensions — from precise logic to playful visuals, from theoretical concepts to physical implementations.

This is not a resume. It is a map of obsessions, experiments, and half-finished ideas that still taught me something valuable. Each universe below contains selected projects, tiny experiments, and fragments that together describe how I think and create.

About

I’m Tasfiq Ibrar , currently studying grade 12 in Faujdarhat Cadet College. Since COVID hit, I had the opportunity to dedicate myself for learning new skills. And now that I look back, I think I have acquired much experience. This experience is what matters to me the most.

I’m a multidisciplinary creator who treats hobbies as research labs. Programming, game development, visual design, arts, crafts, and learning all feed one another — each universe is a lens on the same curiosity.

I’m especially interested in how systems and stories overlap: interfaces that feel like narratives, puzzles that teach, tools that invite play, and small experiments that grow into larger worlds.

Creative Philosophy

Make small, useless things.
Let them redefine you.

I think in universes: each hobby is a space with its own rules, tools, and materials. Instead of forcing them into one narrow identity, I let them redefine their limits. It's all about how much effort you put into it.

The through-line is curiosity. I care less about polished outcomes and more about whether a project asks a real question: What if? How else? Who is this for? This portfolio is a snapshot of those questions in motion.
